Mug Club · $150 a year

Your mug.
Our wall.

150 hand-thrown mugs hang over the bar, each one with a number and a regular. Here's what membership gets you, and a calculator to see if it's worth it.

  • A 20 oz mug for a pint price

    Handmade by a potter in Englewood, numbered, and hung on our wall. Four free ounces every pour.

  • $1 off, every time

    Every pour, every day, including happy hour.

  • First dibs on releases

    Bottle releases open to members an hour early, and a free pour of every new beer on Wednesdays.

  • Member brew day

    Once a year the club brews a beer with Maya. Members vote on the style and name it.

  • Birthday pint

    On us, the whole month of your birthday.

  • Members-only party

    Every December, with a keg of something we won't pour for anyone else.

The fine print

Renews every January.

Membership runs January through December. Miss a renewal and your mug comes off the wall (you keep it). Members must be 21+, and the mug stays at the bar except on the last day of the year.
