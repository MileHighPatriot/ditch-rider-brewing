Events · never a cover

Something on
most nights.

Trivia on Tuesdays, bands on Fridays, a run club that ends with a pint, and a release almost every week. Add anything to your calendar in one tap.

  • Tuesdays · 7pm

    Ditch Trivia

    Teams of up to six. Winners drink free for the rest of the night (well, up to $50). Reserve a table if you're 6+.

  • Wednesdays · 4pm

    Mug Club Pint Night

    Members get a free pour of whatever's newest. Everyone else gets a look at the mugs on the wall.

  • Fridays · 7pm

    Friday Patio Music

    Local bands and songwriters on the patio stage, inside by the tanks when it's cold. Never a cover.

  • Saturdays · 8am

    Canal Run Club

    3 or 5 miles out and back on the High Line Canal trail. Coffee after, and a token for a pint when we open at 11.

  • Sundays · 10am

    Sunday Brunch & Lawn Games

    Breakfast burritos, chilaquiles, and giant Jenga on the lawn. Kids and dogs very welcome.
