The patio

Five-day patio outlook.

Live from Open-Meteo. Fire pits and heaters keep the patio going down to about 40°; we pull the umbrellas when gusts pass 30 mph.

Getting here

Three ways in. One of them has beer at the end.

  • Light rail

    3-minute walk from Orchard Station (E and H lines). Head south on Greenwood Plaza Blvd.

    E and H lines · check RTD for late-night trains

  • Parking

    Free 3-hour parking in the garage behind us. Grab a validation ticket at the bar.

    Rideshare pickup at the front door

  • Bike

    Covered bike racks and a repair stand by the patio gate. The High Line Canal trail is 6 minutes by bike.

    Saturday run club leaves at 8am

House rules

Dogs, kids, and everyone else.

  • Dogs

    Welcome on the patio, on a leash, with water bowls and biscuits at the gate. Colorado health code keeps them out of the taproom, service animals excepted.

  • Kids

    Always welcome with an adult until 9pm. Kids menu, crayons, high chairs, and giant Jenga on the lawn. We'll never serve anyone under 21.

  • Groups

    Walk-ins for up to 8. Bigger than that, call ahead or book the Headgate Room. The after-work rush is 4 to 6.

  • Accessibility

    Step-free entry from the garage, an accessible restroom, and lowered tables inside and on the patio.

Do you take reservations?

For groups of 8 or more, and for the Headgate Room and the patio buyout. Everyone else, walk right in.

Is there a gluten-free beer?

Rider Light is gluten-reduced (under 20 ppm). That's fine for the gluten-shy but not safe for celiac. The kitchen marks gluten-free dishes, though our fryer is shared.

Can I bring my own food?

Birthday cakes, yes, with no fee. Otherwise the kitchen's got you.

What about amphitheater nights?

The patio fills early. Order dinner by 6 and we'll have you out the door by 6:45; it's a 12-minute walk up Greenwood Plaza Blvd.
