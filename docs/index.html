Find us

A block from
Orchard Station.

5690 S Greenwood Plaza Blvd, Greenwood Village, on the Tech Center side of I-25. The after-work crowd shows up around 4; the patio is at its best around sunset.

Hours, patio & parkingDirections

  • Dogs on the patio

    Water bowls and biscuits at the gate. Inside is humans only (Colorado rules, not ours).

  • Kids welcome

    Kids menu, crayons, and lawn games. Minors with an adult until 9pm.

  • 3 min from light rail

    Orchard Station, E and H lines. Ride home, no parking needed.

  • Bike racks & a repair stand

    Six minutes from the High Line Canal trail. Post-ride pint encouraged.
